Source Triage

Eight sources from six disciplines. This time, credibility isn't the only question — who is this source actually for? A systematic review, a shift-handoff note, and a patient handout can all be well-made and still be the wrong fit if they land in front of the wrong audience. Sort each one into where it actually belongs.

ACRL Framework: Authority Is Constructed and Contextual
Intra

Intraprofessional — for colleagues in your own discipline (e.g. PT to PT).

Inter

Interprofessional — for the whole care team, across disciplines.

Public

Public — for patients, families, or the community.

Not credible

Not Credible — shouldn't inform a care plan for any audience.
