Know Your Team

Six situations, one shared question each time: who on the care team should be looped in first? This isn't about who's "in charge" — it's the Roles & Responsibilities competency: knowing your own scope of practice well enough to know whose scope a problem actually falls into.

1. A patient recovering from a stroke has started coughing every time she drinks water.

2. A patient can now walk short distances but says buttoning a shirt and using a fork are still nearly impossible.

3. A patient is medically ready to go home but lives alone on the third floor of a walk-up apartment with no family nearby.

4. A patient with newly diagnosed Type 2 diabetes needs a meal plan that fits their culture, budget, and cooking situation at home.

5. A patient's gait has become unsteady since the last visit, raising concern about a fall risk at home.

6. The team notices three patients from the same apartment complex admitted this month with similar respiratory symptoms.
